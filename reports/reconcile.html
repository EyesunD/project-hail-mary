Methodology

Reconcile answers one question: does our model match the app? App values come from the user-maintained PortfolioValue sheet; our model compounds forward from there and the gap is the model-accuracy signal.

  • App (start) / App (end): sourced from the PortfolioValue sheet's (SGD) columns. Start = earliest snapshot ≤ end_date; end = latest snapshot ≤ end_date. The reconciliation window is the gap between these two dates.
  • Δ Perf: app_end − app_start − deposits — deposit-adjusted real performance per sleeve, in SGD. For new sleeves (start = 0) the % column uses deposits as the denominator so the % is "return on capital invested".
  • Our Model: multi-anchor BH with deposit injection. At the start anchor we allocate observed value × target weights to each holding (fixed shares). We compound forward via Yahoo prices, rebalancing shares at every target-change date. Mid-period deposits are injected at their date — converted to native currency using the explicit Stashaway rate from the Deposits sheet (column "FX Rate to base") when available, else Yahoo midmarket with a 0.2% spread haircut as a default. Stashaway management fees from book_config.MGMT_FEES_ANNUAL are pro-rated daily.
  • Gap: our_model − app_end. The model already includes the cash flows shown in "+ Deposits" — adding them again would double-count.
  • Status badges: exact (<0.05%), ok (<0.3%), noise (<1%), ⚠ investigate (≥1%) — % of app_end.
  • Drill-down (per-portfolio): anchors at every PortfolioValue snapshot date (+ end_date), with a synthetic anchor at the deposit date for sleeves seeded mid-period. Per holding: $-value at each anchor (native currency with SGD subtext for USD sleeves), 1M Δ and Cum Δ. Sleeve footer shows Observed / BH-modelled / DR-modelled (daily-rebalanced) with two drift rows — BH vs Observed (model accuracy) and BH vs DR (rebalancing-assumption effect).
  • Data-integrity banner: book_health_check runs on every regeneration and surfaces untagged portfolios, unknown tickers, weight-sum violations, mid-period deposits and FX staleness as a banner above the main table.
